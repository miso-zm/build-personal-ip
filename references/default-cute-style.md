# Default Cute Illustration Style

Use this house style when the user uploads a real-person or pet photo without supplying a target illustration style.

## Visual direction

- Create a warm, friendly, handmade chibi character suitable for personal branding, stickers, article illustrations, and knowledge cards.
- Use an approximately 2.5–3-head-tall proportion: oversized rounded head, compact torso, short limbs, and small simplified hands and feet.
- Keep the silhouette soft, round, slightly asymmetrical, and immediately readable.
- Use warm dark-brown, slightly hand-wobbly contours with a thin-to-medium, mostly consistent weight.
- Simplify hair into several large flowing masses with a limited number of internal lines. Preserve the real person's visible hairstyle, parting, length, color, and signature highlights.
- Use tiny dark oval or bean-shaped eyes, simple brows, a very small warm-coral curved mouth, a minimal nose, and restrained peach-pink cheek blush.
- Use flat, matte color fills with gentle, clear hues and a limited palette. Favor muted sky blue, warm cream, soft coral, dusty red, pale peach, warm gold, and neutral dark brown when they fit the subject.
- Add only a faint colored-pencil or paper-grain finish. Keep it subtle enough that the silhouette and clothing remain clean.
- Use a pure white or very lightly warm-white background unless the output format requires transparency or a scene.
- Keep clothing construction readable but simplified. Preserve defining collars, cuffs, straps, hems, pockets, and color relationships while removing excessive seams and fabric texture.
- Use small hand-drawn motion marks, tears, sparkles, or emphasis lines only when they support an expression or action.

## Default character mood

Aim for approachable, lively, gentle, and slightly playful. Avoid infant-like proportions, exaggerated anime expressions, or a generic commercial mascot face.

## Avoid

- photorealism or realistic anatomy
- glossy anime shading, 3D rendering, or plastic surfaces
- vector-perfect curves and mechanically identical features
- oversized mouths, huge sparkling eyes, or extreme facial distortion
- dense hair strands, heavy clothing texture, or unnecessary seam detail
- muddy vintage filters, gray-brown casts, or very low contrast
- thick wax-crayon blocks, mottled paint, or noisy paper texture
- copying the identity, outfit, accessories, or props of unrelated reference characters

## Prompt core

```text
Render the user's visible identity as a warm handmade chibi illustration: approximately
2.5–3 heads tall, rounded soft silhouette, warm dark-brown lightly wobbly outlines,
simple bean-shaped eyes, a tiny warm-coral mouth, restrained peach blush, simplified
hair masses, clean matte colors, subtle colored-pencil paper grain, and a pure white or
light warm-white background. Preserve the user's identity and signature traits; avoid
photorealism, glossy anime shading, 3D rendering, dense detail, and oversized facial features.
```
